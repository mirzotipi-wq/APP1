# Launch and commercial validation

The target is $1,000 USD developer proceeds in first 30 days, not a forecast. Proposed $29.99 one-time unlock; 40 sales exceed $1,000 after assumed 15% commission; 50 sales produce about $1,275 before other expenses. At 30% commission, 50 sales produce about $1,050. Actual taxes, refunds, eligibility, FX, fees, marketing and payout timing vary. Validate name, trademark, App Store availability, pricing and rules before publication.

Before investing heavily: 30 interviews about existing lighting workflow and real frustrations; 500 qualified landing page visits, target 100 waitlist signups; 50 TestFlight users, at least 20 willing to pay the proposed price. Record actual consent and anonymous aggregate findings. Do not scrape personal data or send mass unsolicited messages. Test competitor workflows with lawful access; log date and source. Change scope if people like demos but won't pay.

Launch content: real one-light portrait, three-light setup, small room recreation, before/after comparison, AR positioning, printable crew diagram. Publish only owner-approved content with rights and truthful capabilities. Reach photographers through tutorials, relevant communities following their rules, educators and opt-in creator outreach. Track install-to-first-project, first-project-to-export, trial/paywall-to-purchase, refunds and support feedback with privacy-preserving measures.

App Store prep: provisional name clearance, developer membership, signing, App Store Connect product, screenshots from actual build, privacy labels and policy, support URL, accessibility, review notes, sandbox tests, TestFlight feedback and explicit owner release approval. No agent may activate purchases, make charges or publish without permission.

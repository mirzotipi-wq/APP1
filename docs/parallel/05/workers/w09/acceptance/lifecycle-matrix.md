## Acceptance limits

Hit lookup establishes current graph ownership, independently of permission to dispatch a selection callback. In particular, a retained RealityKit graph can still resolve its owned hits while its anchors are off-window, the surface is disabled, or its coordinator has dismantled view attachments. Coordinator rendering/window/current-scene/callback gates prevent delivery; removed or invalidated lamp ownership still rejects stale hits. SceneKit disable/off-window gating likewise belongs to the handler, while its dismantle clears hit maps/content availability.

All source mutations and adapter callbacks are main-actor synchronous. That reduces interleaving in the inspected call paths; it does not measure renderer-thread behavior or delivered recognizer timing. A disabled/off-window/dismantled guard is meaningful at the adapter boundary; caller generation/live-UUID guards remain necessary for project identity.

## Evidence accounting

The source inventory was independently enumerated using the method-discovery pattern in `scripts/native_test_shards.py`: **184 native / 38 spatial methods** (`source_inventory.json`). Counts describe source declarations, not passes. Previous source9725 run45826141504 passed176 native /30 spatial, including actual SceneKit projections and original ordinary/crowded snapshots. The four navigation-recovery, three selection-lifetime and one aim-recovery methods plus RealityKit rectangle-face correction are outside that earlier SHA. Current run45838950713 is coordinator-owned; final results must be cited through its exact receipt.

All eight bounding-box corner projections exercise the supplied bounds. They do not independently prove that every primitive is inside those bounds. Wave08's geometry and implementation roles separately derive displayed primitive envelopes, including outline thickness, markers and camera/bust geometry, to close that logical gap at source/portable level. They cannot establish native mesh tessellation, renderer culling, gesture delivery or physical-device behavior.

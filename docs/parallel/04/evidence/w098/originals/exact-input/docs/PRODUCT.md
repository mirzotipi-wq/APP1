# Product contract — v1

## Promise
Key & Fill enables photographers and filmmakers to turn a visual reference into an editable, practical lighting plan in minutes. It is not a replacement for a light meter or a physically exact inverse-rendering system.

Audience: solo portrait photographers, photography students, independent cinematographers, small crews, YouTubers. First wedge: indoor one-to-three-light portraits. Job: reduce trial and error, communicate setups, reproduce looks with equipment users own.

## First release workflow
1. Create a project or open a starter recipe. Import an authorized reference image using PhotosPicker; explain on-device behavior.
2. Provide subject/camera/room context and optionally identify light sources; suggest one to three plausible rigs with uncertainty, including a manual mode.
3. Edit lights, positions, height, aiming, output, color temperature and modifiers in a native real-time 3D studio. Orbit/pan/zoom, top view, camera preview; selection, precise numerical entry, undo/redo.
4. Preview relative changes and compare test image side-by-side. Explicitly distinguish simulated light from physical calibration.
5. Place anchors with AR; show camera, subject, lights and distances; offer non-AR measurements where unsupported. Handle tracking loss and reset.
6. Save locally, reopen, duplicate and export a readable lighting diagram with equipment list, distances, settings, assumptions and version.
7. Free mode offers genuine basic workflows; proposed Pro lifetime nonconsumable at $29.99 USD unlocks unlimited projects/advanced editing, AR and exports. Exact entitlements, price and launch must be owner-approved. Never gate previously purchased work unfairly or lose user data.

## Scope and constraints
Portraits first, single room, modest light counts; no full movie-production suite, social network, desktop clone, exact reconstruction promise, mandatory account, paid external service, or unlimited cloud inference in 1.0. RoomPlan/LiDAR is optional; manual room setup and capability fallbacks remain useful. Support portrait and landscape where editing benefits. English first; localization-ready strings and metric/imperial display with internal SI units.

## Success hypotheses
Target 50 paid sales in first 30 days after launch. At $29.99, gross $1,499.50; hypothetical proceeds $1,274.58 at 15% commission before tax/refunds/costs. Small Business eligibility is not assumed. Validate pricing and demand before major expenditure; revenue is not a software quality metric and not guaranteed.

# Technical architecture

## Platform
Native SwiftUI iOS 17+ baseline; verify deployment and toolchain requirements before release. Portable LightingCore Swift package for domain logic. App/ is the composition/UI layer. XcodeGen produces a reproducible Xcode project. Prefer Apple frameworks, limited dependencies with documented licenses, no embedded keys.

## Boundaries
- LightingCore: value-type scene, SI units, validation, ideal photometry, deterministic fixtures. Current ideal emitter frames, directional/rectangular photometry, camera geometry, ideal Planckian/Bradford color pipeline, relative neutral-card exposure and schema v5 with optional measured-room settings and rectangular-emitter roll; future modules include calibrated camera response/color/exposure, equipment distribution and solver contracts. No Apple UI/AR dependency.
- Studio rendering: RealityKit for scene interaction/AR shared representation; use Metal custom shading where necessary for physical fidelity. Keep adapter separate from domain; use scene-linear radiance, explicit exposure/white balance/tone mapping and consistent coordinate transforms. Distinguish candela, lumen, lux and electrical watts. Do not equate watts with luminous output; calibrated equipment profiles include provenance and uncertainty. Area-source integration/soft shadows require numerical validation; point-source estimate is not a softbox simulator. Current Study CPU oracle traces a neutral sphere and finite panel, with direct diffuse/GGX response and deterministic 8×8 or 32×32 finite-emitter samples, visibility, and relative color. An optional Metal Float32 kernel consumes the same validated packed sample/camera/white-balance data; runtime parity checks six known fixtures before selecting it and falls back to the Double CPU oracle. A portable C++ harness executes the shared math against the Swift oracle, not the actual GPU. Study also offers a session-only neutral mannequin: original continuous 5248-triangle head, bounded BVH, analytic ears/neck/clothing/panel, smooth outward normals with geometric fallback at grazing views, geometric shadow offset and front-hemisphere checks. Uses same ideal direct-light samples, camera, exposure and color transform; CPU-only 144×96 maximum, no mesh Metal parity claim. It is synthetic study geometry, not photo reconstruction or calibrated skin. Sphere GPU/CPU path remains distinct. A four-entry LRU retains completed Study frames with at most 256 KiB of RGBA payload plus at most 64 KiB of validity masks; validated cache keys omit display names, cancelled/failed renders are not retained, and a backend downgrade evicts cached Metal frames. The 3D studio now uses RealityStudioPreview and StudioEntityGraph for RealityKit orbit, pan, zoom and light selection; Intel iOS simulators select the existing SpatialPreview/SceneKit orbit and Fit renderer before constructing any RealityKit resources, avoiding the observed CoreRE initialization trap while retaining real geometry and light selection. The overhead plan remains SwiftUI and the authored camera preview retains SpatialPreview/SceneKit. These views remain illustrative; realistic subject/materials, calibration and physical-device interaction/performance acceptance remain outstanding.
- Editor: SwiftUI state on main actor, stable IDs, commands/undo, normalized values, selection, inspector, accessible numeric controls; move expensive work off main actor, cancel stale tasks.
- Reference: PhotosPicker supplies user-selected images normalized to bounded, metadata-stripped sRGB derivatives. ReferenceAnalysisService uses on-device Vision face/pose checks and linearized image luminance for a tentative key-side cue, with explicit ambiguity and manual fallback; applying a proposed rig requires user acceptance. PhotoComparisonView provides side-by-side and wipe comparison of session-only reference/test photos. Model output is untrusted; validate ranges, rank multiple plausible rigs, abstain on ambiguity. Held-out inference and physical-device privacy acceptance remain outstanding. Never infer identity or upload images by default.
- AR: ARPlacementController and ARPlacementView implement ARKit world tracking and RealityKit placement at authored 1:1 scale, with explicit transforms, floor placement, yaw, capability checks, user-started camera access, tracking status, reset and manual measurement fallback. Lifecycle/recovery and placement accuracy still require physical-device acceptance. RoomPlan remains optional on supported LiDAR devices. Do not invent placement accuracy.
- Persistence: versioned Codable JSON, atomic writes, app-private storage; current versioned projects.json snapshot with validated stable project IDs, active ID, 64 projects and 8 MiB bound, idempotent legacy portrait.json migration, optimistic stale-snapshot protection, reversible archive and confirmed archived-record deletion. In-process load/migration and compare-and-replace are serialized under one lock; external writers are outside the contract. All three concurrency regressions passed Debug and Release at f46ec3b7; current native verification remains required. Editable project backup/import uses bounded validated reads. The project library supports search and saved-rig thumbnails; recovery compares the complete observed library snapshot before replacing it, preserving unrelated records and active identity. Device provider behavior and broader acceptance remain outstanding; host write-failure and interruption evidence is recorded separately from physical-device evidence. No silent overwrite on decode failure.
- Commerce: EntitlementStore implements StoreKit 2 nonconsumable product lookup, verified entitlements, transaction updates, purchase and explicit restore. The product identifier defaults to blank, disabling sales; current features remain free and displayed prices come from StoreKit. Product configuration and free/Pro boundaries require owner approval. Sandbox purchase, restore, cancellation, pending, revocation and offline acceptance remain outstanding. No paywall gating before valid implementation.
- Export: local vector PDF and text plans include equipment settings, units and committed room measurements; PDF footers identify report and scene versions. Editable project backups retain scene settings, including measured rooms and emitter roll, while excluding reference/test photos and undo history. System sharing/file export is user-initiated. Source provenance, readable accessible legends and native share/open, provider and device acceptance remain required.

## Coordinates and color
Right-handed scene coordinates, meters: +x right, +y up, +z toward camera for initial default; view adapters handle transforms explicitly. Orientation conventions must be documented and unit-tested. Point source ideal illuminance E = I cos(theta) / r² on a surface, with bounded physically valid domain; implemented with explicit receiver orientation and normalized direction, cosine directional falloff and bounded finite Lambertian rectangle integration. The diagnostic lux meter retains the 32x32 midpoint convention for fully front-facing rectangles, clips partial positive-incidence regions, and uses 12x12 Gauss-Legendre integration per triangle (maximum 432 evaluations); separation remains at least 5 cm and one-quarter of the longest side. The CPU/shared-Metal Study model retains the midpoint grid on fully illuminated faces. At grazing angles it clips the positive-incidence domain (and the geometric front hemisphere where required), then redistributes the same bounded sample budget across columns using illuminated area and centroid weights. Per-sample visibility and material evaluation remain active. This continuous partial-face approximation differs from the diagnostic meter quadrature; it is not real-world calibration. CPU and compute images retain explicit per-pixel unsupported masks, and parity compares both mask locations and display values. Camera uses 36 × 24 mm sensor, 14–200 mm focal length, local forward −Z and ideal perspective. v1–v4 scenes migrate to v5 with legacy optics, lens and default card settings preserved; optional measured-room and emitter-roll values remain absent for those legacy versions. Light spectra are ideal blackbodies normalized to photopic Y; direct lux-weighted XYZ is transformed with Bradford white adaptation, relative stop scaling, luminance Reinhard tone mapping and neutral-axis gamut compression to sRGB. The card is a diagnostic 18% reflectance with a 1000 lx reference, not a measured camera response; relative exposure does not change lux. Current SceneKit adapter approximates lighting and requests the ideal vertical FOV in a 3:2 viewport; neither rendered photometry nor native framing output is calibrated or verified. Color temperature in Kelvin is not a complete spectral model. Keep photometric calculations separate from RGB display and test exposure and monotonicity.

## Security
Use least privilege, clear permission purposes, no analytics by default, no secret logging, no photo data in telemetry. Treat imported files and model output as untrusted; bound input sizes, validate schema, handle corruption and cancellation. External inference is an explicit future decision with consent, data retention and cost limits.

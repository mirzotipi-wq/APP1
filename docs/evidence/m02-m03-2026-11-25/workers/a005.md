## Defect and correction

SceneKit constructs the rectangular fixture as a 0.055 m deep `SCNBox` centered at the authored light position. Its local +Z face therefore lies 0.0275 m toward the target from the authored emitting plane. `EmitterFrame` already supplies the correct complete basis, including authored roll and deterministic vertical aims.

After applying that existing basis, the candidate translates each aimed box backward along its local Z axis by half `box.length`. The positive-Z front face now coincides with the authored source position. Dimensions, chamfer, orientation, materials, hierarchy, source node, stand anchor, and selection registration retain their existing behavior. The correction runs in both orbit fallback and authored camera preview.

The change also treats the directional fixture consistently. Its 0.13 m deep box previously placed its front 0.065 m ahead of the authored source. Although the directional photometry is an ideal cosine point emitter rather than a finite area, its visible fixture has an aimed front, and `StudioEntityGraph` already places the visible front at the authored position for both directional and rectangular kinds. Moving the directional housing back by 0.065 m gives both adapters the same spatial meaning without changing directional photometry or the SceneKit light node.

Bare-point markers remain spheres centered on the authored source. `EmitterKind` has exactly `point`, `directional`, and `rectangle`; there is no disc case to modify or certify. An aimed light with a degenerate source/target has no `EmitterFrame`: its existing centered, selectable marker is preserved and no spot/omni source is invented.

No new production visibility or helper API was necessary. Tests inspect actual SceneKit nodes through the existing native `makeView`/`updateView` seam and coordinator state.

## Added native acceptance coverage

Five new XCTest methods are included automatically by the existing `sources: [NativeTests]` target:

1. `testRectangularFrontPlaneAndRollMatchAuthoredFrameInBothPreviewModes`: compares SceneKit front-face center and all three basis vectors against the domain frame at a translated rig, using oblique, vertical-up, and vertical-down aims with nonzero rolls in orbit and camera modes. Also checks authored width/height and the spotlight's unchanged position and negative-Z emission direction.
2. `testDirectionalHousingFrontUsesAuthoredPlaneEvenWhenPoweredOff`: checks directional face alignment and the unchanged zero-intensity light source position.
3. `testBarePointAndOmnidirectionalSourceKeepAuthoredCenter`: checks that point geometry/source remain centered despite irrelevant aim/roll values and retain selectable identity.
4. `testOffsetHousingPreservesFixtureStandAndSelectionIdentity`: checks fixture and stand mappings, selection-outline ancestry, original stand center, and geometry identity through select/deselect updates.
5. `testDegenerateAimKeepsSelectableMarkerWithoutInventingEmitterDirection`: checks centered marker behavior, selection mapping, and absence of active source nodes when no frame exists.

The geometry assertions allow Float32 transform precision (20 micrometers for world positions, including x = 80 m / z = 75 m), far below the original 27.5 mm rectangular displacement. They establish geometric adapter consistency only; no screenshot, rendered shading, actual tap delivery, physical calibration, or device-performance result is claimed.

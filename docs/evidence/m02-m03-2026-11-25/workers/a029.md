# Worker 29 — bounded compiler-inlining experiment for grazing study cost

## Disposition

**No optimization retained.** A single source-level compiler-specialization pass did not demonstrate a sound material improvement against the frozen a028 grazing candidate. All three experimental `@inline(__always)` attributes were removed, and `Sources/LightingCore/ReferenceStudy.swift` was restored byte-for-byte to its starting contents, including the root's corrected sampling comments. The a028 report's measured 17.56–35.10% area-frame cost versus the pre-grazing baseline remains unresolved; this experiment must not be presented as a performance fix.

The experiment preserved all eight diagnostic image hashes and all four receiver signal sums exactly. Both variants returned cancellation in all 12 probes. Timing was mixed and noisy: three of four area-frame medians increased, one decreased with overlapping sample ranges, and all four point-frame medians increased. The area receiver microbenchmarks improved 7.76% without visibility and 0.66% with visibility in this pair. That does not justify retaining an attribute change that has no consistent area-frame benefit.

## Scope, hypothesis, and exact source identity

The owned repository path was solely `Sources/LightingCore/ReferenceStudy.swift`. No shared header, test, manifest, other application source, `.build`, commit, or push was modified by this worker.

The hypothesis was that forcing inlining at the sampler, polygon clip helper, and captured accumulation helper would expose the closure body and fixed axis clipping planes to the Swift optimizer, reducing call/closure overhead. The sole attempted patch added `@inline(__always)` to:

- `StudyAreaSliceSampling.forEach`
- `StudyAreaSliceSampling.clip`
- Local `accumulate` inside `StudyLighting.evaluate`

No expressions, branches, thresholds, domains, grid dimensions, column moments, sample coordinates, weights, visibility calls, full-face order, or cancellation polling sites were changed. No approximations, adaptive grids, alternate algorithms, or discontinuous switches were attempted. A source-level annotation alone is not proof of a runtime benefit; the real diagnostic decided whether to retain it.

## Interpretation and handoff

Reject the inlining experiment. The evidence does not establish a reliable material area-frame gain, and retaining it would add forced compiler behavior without a demonstrated product benefit. The frozen grazing implementation remains as measured in a028, including its CPU cost. A future performance task should identify time spent in clipping versus per-sample evaluation before further changes; that work is outside this bounded pass. No performance acceptance threshold was invented or weakened.

These measurements are a short Linux shared-host diagnostic, not physical-device, Metal/GPU, memory, thermal, sustained-frame-rate, or release-gate evidence. This pair also does not prove the inlining annotations intrinsically slow the renderer; host noise and the single fixed ordering limit causal inference. It does prove that this authorized pass did not produce sound evidence for keeping them.

# A009 — Spatial emitter geometry

**Approved by static review; no blocker found.**

`SpatialPreview.swift:291–296` sets the fixture orientation before `localTranslate`, so the offset follows its rotated local −Z axis. With housing depth d, its center becomes p − normal·d/2 and its local +Z face remains at authored p. Source and stand anchors remain unchanged. This agrees with `StudioEntityGraph.swift:253–268`, whose +Z emitting face terminates at the authored origin and whose housing extends behind it.

Picking preserves the registered fixture node; outline hits still ascend to that fixture, and stand registration remains intact. Selection-only updates retain geometry and toggle the existing indicator.

The native tests cover tilted/vertical aim, roll, both preview modes, directional/off and point cases, identity, selection toggles, and degenerate aim. **Limitation:** no native build or tests were run; actual screen-space hit testing and gesture callbacks remain unverified.

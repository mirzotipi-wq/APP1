## Falsification and findings

- Every finite-area near-field guard executes before the shortcut. A later unsupported point still reaches its original distance check; skipping one rectangle cannot hide a different unsupported rectangle.
- The strict negative depth threshold preserves coplanar/front-facing paths. Its extra 1 nm exceeds rounding at the validated ±100 m coordinates and the small frame-orthogonality residual near the vertical-basis fallback. The additional 5 cm prevents skipping a per-sample distance rejection at its boundary.
- Rolled right/up vectors remain in the emitting plane to that accuracy. Both midpoint and clipped samples therefore have zero emission on the skipped side, independently of receiver or geometric normal.
- Construction stores exactly `grid * grid` contiguous samples per active rectangle. Advancing by that count preserves following point/area blocks and their accumulation order. Pixel cancellation remains outside this loop.
- The six new test methods cover darkness, coplanar/front/grazing results, unsupported mixed rigs, sample ordering and both renderers' cancellation. No existing test or tolerance is weakened.

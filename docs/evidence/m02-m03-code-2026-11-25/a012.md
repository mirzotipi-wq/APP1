Invalid intensity or positions abstain with `nil`.
- `LambertianRectangle.init` restricts sides to 0.05…4 m, intensity to finite 0…100,000 cd, and basis orthogonality to `1e-8`. `rectangularIlluminance` requires absolute plane separation of `max(0.05, longestSide/4)` before returning any physical zero. Backfaces return zero; unsupported near-plane geometry returns `nil`. Fully contributing faces use 32×32 midpoint integration; partial faces use clipped positive incidence and 12×12 quadrature per triangle. Both evaluate Lambertian emission and receiver cosine with inverse-square distance and area-normalized radiance.
- `SurfacePhotometryTests.testRectangleMatchesIndependentOffAxisNumericalReference` declares four offset/tilted cases with `<0.05` relative-error assertions against independent Simpson integration. Six centered analytic cases use `<0.01`; narrow-strip/corner and partial/full transition checks use `<0.002`. These assertions establish the intended fixture scope, not a universal 5% guarantee or an executed pass. `PhotometryInvariantTests` covers rigid transforms, similar-source scaling, reciprocity, and mixed-rig yaw/translation.
- `LightOutputAdjustment.light` validates finite nonnegative targets, source and scene; positive targets require positive finite one-candela lux. It rejects unsupported geometry, out-of-range outputs and achieved/target ratios outside `1e-9`. Zero deliberately switches output off without sampling geometry, as documented. Its unchanged-output boundary fallback requires exact original lux equality. No inconsistent lux adjustment or silently accepted unsupported geometry was identified within the reviewed surface domain.

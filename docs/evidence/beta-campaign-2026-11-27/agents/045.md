## Change and justification

The panel guard uses subject-relative origin O, height H=O.y+radius, and unnormalized ray Q. Each finite-panel edge is the homogeneous inequality `(-Q.y)*(extent-sign*O.axis)-sign*H*Q.axis >= 0`, avoiding an ill-conditioned division near horizontal rays. Explicit componentwise Float32 engineering allowances are propagated through the product expansion. On each image row, five linear constraints produce outer possible-hit and inner guaranteed-hit intervals; integer pixel counts give the uncertain boundary shell in O(image height) work.

Panel and sphere uncertainty now share the existing 1/510 pixel allowance, reserving at most half an 8-bit mean channel level for those possible discontinuities under the declared bands. The factor is an engineering allowance, not a proved universal GPU error bound. Overlap, sphere occlusion and near clipping can cause conservative extra CPU selection.

All 15 original panel-edge scenes now route to CPU, including three whose raw host comparison already passed. Packed inputs and Double images remain unchanged: all 15 binary fixtures and 15 scene JSONs match 044 byte for byte. Kernel, raw validation fixtures, parity thresholds, resolution limits and supported scene bounds are unchanged. Ordinary portrait/startup/wide rotated views remain eligible; routing remains specific to the requested grid.

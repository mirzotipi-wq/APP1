## Color validation

- Approximate blackbody 2856 K: x=0.44623356243897605, y=0.408660536585054. CIE illuminant A nominal x≈0.44757, y≈0.40745; absolute errors 0.00134 and 0.00121 against a deliberately bounded 0.003 tolerance.
- 6500 K blackbody: x=0.31341916244577267, y=0.3238982606849196. This is not CIE D65.
- 45 combinations of source temperature, white balance and exposure extremes stayed finite and in display gamut. Matched Planckian white is neutral; one stop matches doubling the signal, not incident lux. Scene-linear XYZ adds before mapping. Invalid scenes and unsupported active geometry abstain; off lights contribute zero.
- Relative 18% card is not spectral rendering, material appearance, real camera exposure, or a measured fixture profile. The existing SceneKit shading is unchanged by card settings.

## Methods and provenance

- Wyman, Sloan & Shirley, 2013, [Simple Analytic Approximations to the CIE XYZ Color Matching Functions](https://jcgt.org/published/0002/02/01/), Eq. 4 mathematical coefficients. Independent Swift implementation, not bundled external source.
- [IES Planck radiation law](https://ies.org/definitions/planck-radiation-law/); 380–780 nm, 5 nm trapezoidal integration, Y-normalized; no actual lamp SPD.
- [CIE colorimetric data registry](https://registry.color.org/colorimetry-data/) and [standard illuminant chromaticity reference](https://www.colour-science.org/api/0.3.1/html/_modules/colour/colorimetry/dataset/illuminants/chromaticity_coordinates.html).
- [ICC Bradford adaptation reference](https://archive.color.org/files/ICC_Minor_Revision_for_Web.pdf); [W3C CSS Color 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color/) sRGB D65 conversion/encoding. Tone map and neutral-axis compression are explicit preview design choices.
- [GitHub Ubuntu runner image](https://github.com/actions/runner-images/blob/main/images/ubuntu/Ubuntu2404-Readme.md). Standard Ubuntu job now supplies independent domain and syntax evidence; macOS remains mandatory for native build and smoke.

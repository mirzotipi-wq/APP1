## Disposition

**No demonstrable defect in the assigned navigation/recovery paths.** No patch or additional tests warranted.

`App/SpatialViewportGeometry.swift` validates scene bounds before fitting. Orbit ignores nonfinite samples and bounds pitch; pan rejects invalid viewport sizes and nonfinite computed coordinates before committing, then bounds the target; zoom rejects invalid/nonpositive scales and clamps distance. Extreme finite inputs therefore cannot poison navigation. The projection helper rejects zero/nonfinite dimensions and Float-singular near-180° angles. Fit restores a deterministic camera and target, with clipping depth enclosing the authored bounds.

`RealityStudioPreview.swift:229–269` defers a pending Fit until both a usable viewport projection and valid scene exist. Resizing updates projection without resetting orbit; ordinary scene edits do not request another Fit. Invalid scenes disable interaction while retaining recovery state. Navigation updates only the adapter camera, never authored scene values.

`SpatialPreview.swift:205–289, 523–533` keeps the last successfully applied mode across an invalid scene, so a subsequent valid orbit transition still performs its pending Fit. Undefined authored camera framing clears `pointOfView` and shows an explicit unavailable state; valid recovery restores the authored view. Ordinary edits/resize preserve user navigation, while explicit Fit resets inertia and target.

Existing `SpatialViewportStateTests`, `SpatialNavigationRecoveryTests` and relevant `SpatialPreviewTests` directly cover invalid/tiny geometry, finite extremes, Fit restoration, resize/projection, invalid-mode recovery and preservation of a user camera/target.
